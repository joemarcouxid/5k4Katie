Read me first 💛
  • Jog = slow. Go slow enough that you could talk in full sentences. If you're gasping, slow down — walking is fine too.
  • Every workout starts with a 5-minute brisk walk and ends with a 5-minute easy walk. The app handles it.
  • Rest a day between workouts (e.g. Mon / Wed / Sat). Rest days are when you get stronger.
  • Repeat a week any time it felt too hard. There's no rush — finishing matters more than speed.
  • Sore is OK, sharp pain is not. Stop and rest if something hurts sharply or keeps hurting.
  • Shoes matter. A proper pair of running shoes from a running store makes a big difference.
  • If you have any heart, blood pressure, or joint concerns, check with your doctor before starting.
  • The app talks and beeps at each change, so you can keep your phone in your pocket. Turn the volume up!
